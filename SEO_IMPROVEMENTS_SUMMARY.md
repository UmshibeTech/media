# SEO and Enquiry Improvements

Website: https://www.mshibeweddingfilms.co.za/

## What is in place

- The site has a canonical homepage, page title and description, social sharing metadata, and a `robots.txt` that allows crawling.
- The sitemap lists the homepage. The site is a single-page website, so section links such as `/#services` are not separate pages to submit.
- JSON-LD describes the business and its wedding photography and film service. Structured data can help search engines understand the site but does not guarantee enhanced results or higher rankings.
- The portfolio now leads into a clear option to request a full-film sample, without presenting unverified testimonials.

## What still needs to be done

1. Verify the website in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://www.mshibeweddingfilms.co.za/sitemap.xml`.
3. Use URL Inspection to request indexing and review the homepage's status.
4. Monitor the Search results report for actual impressions, clicks, queries, and page issues.
5. If eligible, create or claim a Google Business Profile with accurate business details. Ask past clients for honest reviews without offering incentives.

Search visibility and client enquiries take time and are not guaranteed. Track enquiries and bookings as well as search traffic to see which marketing channels are working.
